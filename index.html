Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No, but you need to be willing to run a command in a terminal and read a script without panicking. If you've had AI write you something and then run it, you're the right reader.

Which AI tool is this for?

All of them. These are conventions for the code and files the AI produces, not for the AI itself. I use Claude; nothing here depends on that.

Is this just folder organization?

Partly, and I'd rather say so than oversell. Modules 01 through 03 are structure. Modules 04 and 08 are the ones that stop you losing data and catch numbers that are quietly wrong — those are the ones people email me about.

Will this make me a developer?

No. It makes you a competent operator of things a developer would look at and find reasonable. Different skill, genuinely useful, much faster to acquire.
